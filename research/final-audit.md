## Minor editorial/source notes

1. **Legacy totals and exact perk gates are not official-client guarantees.** The prose generally marks current client extraction and uncertainty correctly. Preserve that distinction wherever the category table (65 total), exact tree gates, challenge checklists, or perk values are condensed for the final guide. Blizzard's announcement supports the system and advertised challenges; the exact current costs/prereqs come from secondary client-data tools.

2. **Keep the Divine Grace source conflict attached to the cooldown wherever a short summary repeats the ability.** The detailed sections do this well. A standalone quick-reference table or macro caption should not make “10 minutes” look like an official Blizzard-confirmed correction: official September article says five, current Wowhead spell record says ten, and the live spellbook remains the decisive check.

3. **Current-client data is stronger for launch mechanics than older community guide snapshots.** The 03 source ledger appropriately flags the stale WowFdb Binding Heal field. In any polished consolidation, ensure other older calculator labels do not leak into current wording; current tree names and the November 2 change log should control.

4. **PvP rank timing and spending.** The shadow/PvP chapter correctly avoids promising a fixed Rank 14 schedule and keeps exact current costs subject to the live vendor. For the new dedicated PvP progression passage, use the official Blizzard progression article for currency sources, seasonal tasks, weekly cap, gear set structure, and Emboldened Seal requirements; avoid quoting exact costs unless transcribed from that source and rechecked.

5. **Beta-derived item advice remains appropriately scoped.** The healing chapter distinguishes level-20/30 evidence and does not turn it into a universal 60 BiS list. Preserve those labels if item examples are shortened or moved.

No author files were modified. Source-backed checks included the latest Blizzard forum notes (notably Priest change lines and Discipline row moves), the Blizzard class overview, current talent records/search-indexed client data, the chapter point tables, and source-ledger consistency.
